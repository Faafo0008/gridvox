GRID VOX - vocal tracking chain (VST3)
======================================

A vocal processing plug-in: low cut, gate, mud/grace clean-up, four-band tone EQ,
de-esser, warmth, fast + smooth dynamics, room/plate/hall reverb, output limiter.

INSTALLED FILES
  <VST3 folder>\GRID VOX.vst3   the plug-in bundle
  <VST3 folder>\GRID VOX\       this read-me and the licence notes

AFTER INSTALLING
  Restart the DAW, or rescan plug-ins:
    FL Studio : Options > Manage plug-ins > Find installed plug-ins
    REAPER    : Options > Preferences > Plug-ins > VST > Re-scan
  The plug-in appears as "GRID VOX" (Grid Audio).

USING IT
  * drag a knob vertically to change a value
  * hold SHIFT while dragging for fine control
  * double-click a knob to reset it to its default
  * right-click a knob for a reset menu
  * drag the bottom-right corner of the window to resize (aspect ratio is locked)
  * preset list: CLEAN TRACKING (default), WARM VOCAL, BRIGHT VOCAL, SMOOTH VOICE,
    BROADCAST - use the dropdown or the arrow buttons

  The plug-in reports 8 samples of latency (2x oversampling in the warmth stage);
  always-on compensation is handled by the host.

UNINSTALL
  Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > "GRID VOX" > Uninstall,
  or run "GRID VOX Uninstall.exe" from the VST3 folder.
